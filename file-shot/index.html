    Drag, aim, upload. Basketball for your files.

    • Revives: every 5 uploads in a row earns one, up to 4 at a time. Use it to keep going when you'd otherwise lose.
    • Timed run: 60 seconds on one clock. Clean swishes add 2 seconds.
